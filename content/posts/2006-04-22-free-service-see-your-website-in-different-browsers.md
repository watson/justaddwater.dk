A free online service called [Browsershots](http://browsershots.org/) has just launched (still beta). You provide it with a URI and it will take real screenshots in different browsers of that page.

![Browsershots.org website screenshot](/wp-content/uploads/2006/04/browsershots.png)

Currently you get screenshots from the following browser brands:

- Epiphany 1.4.8
- Firefox 1.0.4
- Firefox 1.5.0
- Galeon 1.3.20
- Konqueror 3.3
- Mozilla 1.7.8
- MSIE 6.0
- MSIE 7.0 Beta
- Opera 8.50
- Safari 2.0

You can even choose multiple screen resolutions as well.
