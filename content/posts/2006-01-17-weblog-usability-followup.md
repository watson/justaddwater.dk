In the previous "[Mullet-style blog layout and complete archive page](/2006/01/09/mullet-style-blog-layout-and-complete-archive-page/ "Justaddwater.dk: Mullet-style blog layout and complete archive page")" we had a look on the Mullet-style blog page that makes more content available on the fronpage, and also argued for replacing monthly archives with a single archive page.Danish "[brugervenligeblogs.dk](http://www.brugervenligeblogs.dk/2006/01/er-weblogs-brugervenlige.html "brugervenligeblogs.dk: er weblogs brugervenlige?")" pointed me to a research document from July 2005: [Blog Mainstream Market Penetration Likely Limited By Basic Usability Issues](http://www.catalystgroupdesign.com/people/press/blogpress.asp "Catalyst Group Design - Blog Mainstream Market Penetration Likely Limited ") by Catalyst Group Design. The 19 page usability report is available as [PDF (119KB)](http://www.catalystgroupdesign.com/cofactors/upload/Blog_usability_report.pdf "PDF version of Catalyst Group Design: Blog Mainstream Market Penetration Likely Limited By Basic Usability Issues").

> Highlights of the study include:
>
> - No participant understood the mechanisms associated with RSS/subscribing to a blog – not even the minority familiar with the term “RSS."
> - Few participants even recognized that they were on an actual blog – and once they did, had a very different reaction to the information presented.
> - A minority of participants understood how to navigate within the blog itself – with most being confused by areas for recent posts, categories, trackbacks and even the comments and archives functions.

I do not know of this company (which is completely normal, as I'm here in Denmark), but from their [weblog](http://www.catalystgroupdesign.com/cofactors/ "Catalyst Group Design blog") I see that they did work for [CNN Money](http://www.catalystgroupdesign.com/cofactors/?p=152 "Catalyst Group Design blog article: CNNMoney.com Launches!").

The report is published 3 months before Jakob Nielsen focused on [weblog usability mistakes](http://www.useit.com/alertbox/weblogs.html "Weblog Usability: The Top Ten Design Mistakes") in his Alertbox from October 17, 2005. In my opinion, Nielsen's points are more or less a repetition of what NOT to do on websites, on intranets, etc. Not much new here, but I consede that we also make some of the mistakes here on justaddwater.dk
