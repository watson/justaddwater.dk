We just started using MeasureMap and have added it to this blog after [Jared Spool recommended it in a comment](/2006/01/12/blog-software-usability/#comment-67 "Jared spool comment on justaddwater.dk").

Recently I have looked for better ways to display incoming links (referring URLs), Google Analytics can but it's a tedious process. The [WordPress Dashboard can only show some of the incoming links](/2006/01/08/google-analytics-trick-how-to-view-referring-urls/ "Justaddwater.dk: Google Analytics trick: How to view referring URLs").

I must say that this part is done very well in MeasureMap.
