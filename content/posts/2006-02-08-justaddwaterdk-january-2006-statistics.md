### RSS/Atom

Around 15% of our hits come from news feed aggregators. This is a bit lower then previously where it was around 25%. This is mainly due to the high number of referrals we have gotten throughout January. As usually RSS and Atom hits have a certain amount of uncertainty build in since news aggregators checks for updates every x minutes - even if there is none - so that adds to the total as well.
