It is often said that an image is worth a thousand words. New York Times is a good resource for interesting visualization on current topics. The article on [Europe's Web of Debt](http://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2010/05/02/weekinreview/02marsh.html) is rather good at conveying a lot of information on the recent European Debt crisis. The width of the arrows and circles represents size of debt.

[![](/wp-content/uploads/2010/05/nytimes-european-debt-visualization-02marsh-image-custom1-v3-300x296.gif "nytimes european debt visualization 02marsh-image-custom1-v3")](/wp-content/uploads/2010/05/nytimes-european-debt-visualization-02marsh-image-custom1-v3.gif)

Earlier this year New York Times brought another interesting visualization: [A Peek Into Netflix \[rental\] Queues](http://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2010/01/10/nyregion/20100110-netflix-map.html), where you can explore where (by US postnumber) any top 100 movie gets rented. A standard US map is overlaid with a heatmap of the data, and combined with excerpts from the movie reviews. Very amusing.
