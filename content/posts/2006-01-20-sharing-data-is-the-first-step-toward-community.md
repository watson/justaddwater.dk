Via [Del.icio.us popular list](http://del.icio.us/popular "Del.icio.us popular bookmarks list") I found this quote from an [IBM Linux commercial](http://video.google.com/videoplay?docid=-3182711082396344549 "IBM Linux commercial on google video")

> Collecting data is only the first step toward wisdom\
> but sharing data is the first step toward community

This is why we decided to use this blog to share data with you. Recently we've posted about our [November](/2005/12/02/justaddwaterdk-november-2005-statistics/ "Justaddwater.dk November 2005 Statistics") and [December 2005](/2006/01/12/justaddwaterdk-december-2005-statistics/ "Justaddwater.dk December 2005 Statistics") visitor statistics.

#### Origin of quote

A [google search](http://www.google.com/search?q=%22Sharing+data+is+the+first+step+toward+community%22 "Google search:") shows approx. 3.000 occurrences of this quote. [Mandriva Linux](http://mandriva.vmlinuz.ca/index.php/MandrivaCommunity:About "Mandriva Linux community") use the quote on their page. Also some Powerpoint documents show up saying it originates from an IBM – On Demand Business Prodigy Advertisement. But before that, somebody must have said it, right?
