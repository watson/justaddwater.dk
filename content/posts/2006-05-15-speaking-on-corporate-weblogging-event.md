June 13, Thomas and I are invited to speak at an event about corporate weblogs in Copenhagen, Denmark. It's hosted by the [Danish IT Industry Association](http://www.itb.dk/site.asp?p=51), and We'll be speaking on the experiences with this blog and how it relates to our jobs at Capgemini. Also, there will be a chance to hear [Thomas Madsen-Mygdal](http://bootstrapping.net/), [Andreas Johannsen](http://asynkron.dk) and the CEO of Sun, Denmark Peter Rindsig ([who hasn't got a blog](http://www.google.com/search?hl=en&q=%22peter+rindsig%22+blog)).\
If you want to attend the event, you can find more details (in Danish) in [the program](http://www.itb.dk/site.asp?p=853&aid=47 "ITB.dk kalender: corporate weblogs").

In case you missed it, I'm also talking [this friday at an afternoon event with Thomas Snitker](/2006/05/10/speaking-on-usability-and-rapid-prototyping/ "justaddwater.dk: Speaking on usability and rapid prototyping") and [June 1 or 2 on reboot8](/2006/05/11/speaking-at-reboot8-about-rails-and-rapid-prototyping/ "Justaddwater.dk: Speaking at reboot8 about Rails and rapid prototyping").\
