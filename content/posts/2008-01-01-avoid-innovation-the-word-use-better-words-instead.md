Scott Berkun brilliantly notes that the word "innovation" has lost it's meaning. [Stop saying innovation - here’s why](http://www.scottberkun.com/blog/2008/stop-saying-innovation-heres-why/ "Permanent Link to Stop saying innovation - here’s why")

> **Use better words instead.** Often people mean one of **1)** we want new ideas **2)** we want better ideas, **3)** we want big changes **4)** we need to place big bets on new ideas. Great. Any of those short phrases are more powerful and specific than the i-word. Use them instead.

I guess this is a challenge in most software consultancies like Capgemini, which I work for. It's a battlefield where reality and psychology meets the marketing department.

Looking at our own blog: 5 articles mention "[innovation](/?s=innovation)", 6 mentions "[innovative](/?s=innovative)" (including this).

PS. While you are at ScottBerkun's site, check out [Asshole Driven Development](http://www.scottberkun.com/blog/2007/asshole-driven-development/) (ADD) where he defines a lot of great acronyms including Cover Your Ass Engineering (CYAE), Development By Denial (DBD) and the title acronym.
