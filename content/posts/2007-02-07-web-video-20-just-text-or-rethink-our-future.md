Must see [Michael Wesch](http://www.ksu.edu/sasw/anthro/wesch.htm) of Kansas University released a brilliant video on the possibilities of the web.

It's a fantastic intro and eyeopener, and it makes me wonder, if it would be relevant to use at work with clients to give a short intro (because it contains a lot of things I may take for granted that people know).

Originally posted on [YouTube](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6gmP4nk0EOE). A [higher quality version](http://mojiti.com/kan/2024/3313) is available from Mojiti (with comments from viewers, actually). **Michael Wesch encourages anybody to add comments on the Mojiti site.**

**In my search for a complete transcript**, i stumbled upon a [short resume](http://danielabarbosa.blogspot.com/2007/02/web-20-good-explanation-in-less-then-5.html "Daniela Barbosa:  Web 2.0 ... a good explanation in less then 5 minutes") of the final sentences of the movie:

> At the end of the video, it points out that we have to rethink about a couple of things, many which i have been thinking about quite a bit as Web 2.0 is embedded into the enterprise-like issues of copyright, authorship, ethics, governance and privacy among others. The full list that professor Wesch lists as things we need to 'rethink' about is:
>
> - copyright
> - authorship
> - identity
> - ethics
> - aesthetics
> - rhetoric
> - governance
> - privacy
> - commerce
> - love
> - family
> - ourselves
