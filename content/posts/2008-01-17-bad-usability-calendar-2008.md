My Norwegian friend Eidar just wrote to inform me that their company ([Netlife Research](http://www.netliferesearch.no/)) has finally released Bad Usability Calendar 2008. Last year, the calendar got quite popular and even Jakob Nielsen mentioned it in [one of his newsletters.](/2007/02/12/jakob-nielsen-mentions-bad-usability-calendar-in-latest-newsletter/)

This year, I'm more than ever looking forward to use it. The calendar has -- as always -- 12 brilliant examples of how not to build a calendar.

![bad-usability-calendar-2008-september.png](/wp-content/uploads/2008/01/bad-usability-calendar-2008-september.png)

What's even better, the calendar now has it's own dedicated website: [www.badusability.com](http://www.badusability.com), where it's also possible to upload photos of the calendar in the wild.

Like last year, I'll ask [Thomas Baekdal](http://baekdal.com) to work with me on a Danish version... unless somebody else puts it up before us (if you plan to translate it, drop me a note so I don't waste the time).
