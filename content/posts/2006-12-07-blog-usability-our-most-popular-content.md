At Justaddwater, we're introducing a new category: "[Best of Justaddwater](/category/best-of-justaddwater/)". Alexander, the Chief Happiness Officer, came up with a ["best of"-category](http://positivesharing.com/2006/12/my-favorites/), that I think any blog could benefit from. Absolutely a brilliant idea.

At the moment, we want this to completely replace our experiment with a wordpress plugin for measuring popular posts. ([Popularity Contest](http://alexking.org/projects/wordpress/popularity-contest) has a number of bugs that skew the popularity numbers for us). The manual work required for estimating what content is popular is OK with us, as long as it gives a better user experience for you, dear reader.
