Thomas and I are attending [RailsConf Europe](http://europe.railsconf.org/) in London, September 14-15 later this year.

![](http://codefluency.com/files/railsconf/railsconf-europe-attendee.png)

From the [RailsConf website](http://europe.railsconf.org/ "RailsConf Europe"):

> The conference is already *packed* with exciting speakers, including the creator of Rails, [David Heinemeier Hansson](http://www.loudthinking.com/), Pragmatic Programmer [Dave Thomas](http://www.pragmaticbookshelf.com/), best-selling author and passion maven [Kathy Sierra](http://headrush.typepad.com/), Rails core developers [Jamis Buck](http://jamis.jamisbuck.org/), [Marcel Molina, Jr.](http://vernix.org/marcel), [Thomas Fuchs](http://script.aculo.us/), Rails authors and trainers [David Alan Black](http://www.rubypowerandlight.com/), and [Chad Fowler](http://www.chadfowler.com/), [Rake](http://rake.rubyforge.org/) author [Jim Weirich](http://onestepback.org/) and many more to be announced.

Although not mentioned on the website, Europe would be a perfect audience for talks on internationalization, so I'd like to see Jeremy Voorhis' presentation "[Globalizing Rails](http://www.jvoorhis.com/articles/2006/04/14/globalizing-rails-in-yvr "Jeremy Voorhis: Globalizing Rails Wrapup")".

We're looking forward to this conference. As frequent readers here may know, we're using [Ruby on Rails](http://rubyonrails.org) in [Capgemini](http://dk.capgemini.com "Capgemini Denmark") for things like [rapid prototyping of web applications](/2006/04/12/rails-prototyping/ "Justaddwater: Ruby on Rails as rapid prototyping tool "). Also I'm particularly interested in the [business value](/2006/05/16/rails-enterprise-business-perspective/ "Justaddwater.dk: Ruby on Rails for the enterprise: Business perspective") of using it.

Get in touch and drop us a comment below if you're attending. Especially, we'd like to know if other people from Denmark are attending.
