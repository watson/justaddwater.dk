Even with the radical website title, I think it deserves a mention that [UsabilityMustDie](http://www.usabilitymustdie.com/) [celebrates its fifth anniversary](http://usability.typepad.com/confusability/2006/11/talkin_bout_a_p.html). Congratulations to Chris McEvoy! Keep up the good and humorous work.

My favorite of Chris McEvoy's work is the Jakob Nielsen spoof article "[Why Ajax Sucks (most of the time)](http://www.usabilityviews.com/ajaxsucks.html)" where he basically just replaced "Frames" with "Ajax" in the original 1996 article "[Why Frames Suck (Most of the Time)](http://www.useit.com/alertbox/9612.html)".
