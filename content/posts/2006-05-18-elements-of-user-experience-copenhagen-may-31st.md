Just a quick note: I'm attending Jesse James Garrett's event in Copenhagen May 31st, "[The elements of User Experience](http://www.adaptivepath.com/events/2006/may3/)". I'm really looking forward to this, as I'm a big admirer of [Jesse](http://blog.jjg.net/) and [Adaptive Path](http://adaptivepath.com).

You probably know that it was Jesse James Garrett who [coined the term "AJAX"](http://www.adaptivepath.com/publications/essays/archives/000385.php "Adaptive Path: Ajax: A New Approach to Web Applications"), which I think is a way to [connect technology with valuable user experience](/2006/02/08/ajax-businesscase/ "Justaddwater.dk: AJAX businesscase: Reduce development costs and increase usability").

Please drop me a note if you plan to attend. As always, I enjoy exchanging ideas and opinions around usability, web standards, Ruby on Rails and the web in general.
