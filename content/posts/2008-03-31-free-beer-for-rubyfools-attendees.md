I thought I'd better post this before April Fools day tomorrow. My employer, Capgemini has accepted to sponsor the [Copenhagen RubyFools conference](http://jaoo.dk/ruby-cph/conference/) from today and till Wendesday.\
[![ruby_fools_logo_cph.gif](http://justaddwater.dk/wp-content/uploads/2008/03/ruby_fools_logo_cph.gif)](http://jaoo.dk/ruby-cph/conference/ "Ruby Fools conference website")

[![capgemini-logo.gif](http://justaddwater.dk/wp-content/uploads/2008/03/capgemini-logo.gif)](http://dk.capgemini.com/ "Capgemini Denmark website")

The sponsorship involves that Capgemini will sponsor free beer for one hour at the Ruby Cave (social event) tomorrow evening. As far as i know, it starts at 7'o clock. So be there and be thirsty. The more free beer you drink, the better return on investment for Capgemini :)
