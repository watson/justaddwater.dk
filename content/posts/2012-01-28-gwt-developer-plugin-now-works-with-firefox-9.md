(UPDATED) The GWT developer plugin has been updated to works with Firefox 9, a couple of days ago.

Its not yet officially available, but can be downloaded directly from the codebase:\
[google-web-toolkit.googlecode.com/svn/trunk/ prebuilt folder](http://google-web-toolkit.googlecode.com/svn/trunk/plugins/xpcom/prebuilt/) in here  is the [gwt-dev-plugin.xpi](http://google-web-toolkit.googlecode.com/svn/trunk/plugins/xpcom/prebuilt/gwt-dev-plugin.xpi), and it works like a charm :)

Previously i wrote when the plugin was updated to [work with Firefox 7 and 8](/2011/10/10/gwt-developer-plugin-for-firefox-7-is-now-ready/).

More info in the group discussion: "[GWT Developer Plugin for Firefox 9](https://groups.google.com/group/google-web-toolkit/browse_thread/thread/8d9d369fdbfdf87e/98f045c1eb38af0d#cdb775c291719219)"

What timing, as Mozilla is likely to release [Firefox 10 this coming Tuesday](https://wiki.mozilla.org/Releases#Firefox_10). For the sake of web developers that use GWT, i hope the plugin updates can come sooner in the future.
