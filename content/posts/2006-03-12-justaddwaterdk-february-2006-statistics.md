### Browser Versions

Nothing big has happen on the browser front this month. The only trend that seems to still be going strong is that Mozilla Firefox is getting an even bigger marked share each month. The losers this month are Internet Explorer by Microsoft, Safari by Apple and Opera by... ehh Opera Software ;)
