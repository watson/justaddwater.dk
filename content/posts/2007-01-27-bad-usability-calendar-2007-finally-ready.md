I've been waiting for this with excitement: Eidar and the rest of NetLife Research in Norway have finally [released the 2007 version of Bad Usability Calendar](http://www.iallenkelhet.no/bad-usability-calendar-2007-is-here).

Direct download: [Bad Usability Calendar 2007](http://iallenkelhet.no/nedlast/bad_usability_calendar_07_english_A3.pdf) (1.2 MB PDF)

Very nice to see that the calendar actually is released under a Creative Commons license, so you can translate it into you native language as long as you keep the reference to NetLife Research.
