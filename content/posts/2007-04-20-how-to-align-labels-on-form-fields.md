**Form usability revisited.** In case you missed it, Luke Wroblewski (principal designer of Yahoo!), has made a thorough presentation on best practices in form design. The entire 112 page presentation is definitely worth a read. Kind of required read in my work :)

[![form_labelalignment.gif](/wp-content/uploads/2007/04/form_labelalignment.gif)](/wp-content/uploads/2007/04/form_labelalignment.gif "form_labelalignment.gif")

Here is why to prefer top-aligned labels:

> Top-aligned labels tend to reduce completion times (how long it takes to complete a form) the most for familiar data (i.e. address, credit card, etc.) because they only require [a single eye fixation](http://www.uxmatters.com/MT/archives/000107.php) to take in both input label & field. Top-aligned labels also work well for forms that require localization or long labels as there is plenty of horizontal real estate to expand/contract the label without negatively impacting the overall page layout. Top-aligned labels, however, do take up a lot of vertical real estate.
>
> (Luke Wroblewski: [Top, Right or Left Aligned Form Labels](http://www.lukew.com/ff/entry.asp?504))
