Our Norwegian friends at Netlife Research have opened for suggestions to next years Bad Usability Calendar. I already used the [2006 calendar with great impact](/2006/10/16/bad-usability-calendar/ "Justaddwater.dk: Bad Usability Calendar 2006") in my professional engagements, and I'm really looking forward to next year's version.

> To make a calendar for 2007 that has the same level of unusual unusefulness, we would like your feedback.
>
> Give us your best examples of bad usability!
>
> The best idea will get an honourable mention, as well as a numbered limited edition of the 2007 Bad Usability Calendar. All suggestions that make the final cut will get a prize.
>
> If you need inspiration, check out the Bad Usability Calendars for [2005](http://www.netliferesearch.com/files/kalender_netlife_V3_mai2005.jpg)(jpg), and [2006](http://www.netliferesearch.com/files/kalender_2006_eng_A3.pdf) (pdf). Thank you in advance for all your help.

Add your suggestions at their blog IAllEnkelhet.no (which means something like "in all it's simplicity").
