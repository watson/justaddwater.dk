Monday to Tuesday is the Danish usability conference "[Usability dagene 2005](http://danskit.dk/sw3696.asp)" (usability days 2005). I'm looking forward to this, and especially to hear Jacob Nielsen, Rolf Molich and Thomas Snitker.

According to the program, Jacob will talk about\
**Usability Why & How**

- User behavior on the Web
- High-end users vs. less experienced users
- Websites vs. Intranets, Web-based applications vs. ephemeral applications
- Information foraging
- Content usability, how to write for online
- Usability metrics, ROI

I always enjoy Jakob because he's very enthusiastic and has good examples that I usually can apply to my own situation.

Both Thomas Watson and I are attending. If you want to meet up, please contact me on the blog here, call me or send a mail. The photo is from Usability Week 2004 in Copenhagen, where I had the chance to say hi to Jakob Nielsen.
