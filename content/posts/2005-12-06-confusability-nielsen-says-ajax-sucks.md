Via [Ajaxian](http://ajaxian.com/archives/2005/12/jakob_nielsen_w_1.html) I saw an alleged article by Jakob Nielsen "Why Ajax sucks (most of the time)". I jumped right into the joke, and read the Ajaxian post carefully. Normally, Alertboxes are published every two weeks and the latest was published [yesterday](/2005/12/05/usability-testing-videos/).

The [article is available](http://www.usabilityviews.com/ajaxsucks.html) at a website called [usabilityviews](http://www.usabilityviews.com/) (have not seen that before). At the very bottom it says

> This is a spoof article. Please compare it with [the original](http://www.useit.com/alertbox/9612.html) and you will see how little it has been changed.
>
> Constructed by [Chris McEvoy](http://confusability.com) with apologies to [Jakob Nielsen](http://www.usabilityviews.com/userati_jakob_nielsen.html).

Hehe, very funny! Chris McEvoy has posted [an appetizer](http://usability.typepad.com/confusability/2005/12/why_ajax_sucks_.html) on his website. It seems that Ajaxian took the bait! And I'm adding [confusability](http://usability.typepad.com/) to my [Netvibes](http://netvibes.com) rss reader!
