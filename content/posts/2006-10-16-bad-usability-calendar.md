Perfect wallpaper for any project room. The Bad Usability Calendar from Norwegian company [Netlife Research](http://www.netliferesearch.com/).

At UI 11 last week I was fortunate to meet Eidar ([photo](http://flickr.com/photos/jesper/267203608/)) from Netlife Research. His usability firm made a Bad Usability Calendar, which is absolutely fantastic. I'm putting the link here directly, as their pages are in Norwegian.

- **Bad Usability Calendar 2006 ([Direct link, English version, 886 kb PDF](http://www.netliferesearch.com/files/kalender_2006_eng_A3.pdf))**, \[[link to page](http://www.netliferesearch.com/article.php?articleID=170)\]

![Bad usability calendar 2006 screenshot](/wp-content/uploads/2006/10/screenshot-bad-usability-calendar1.png)\
They also did a [similar calender for 2005](http://www.netliferesearch.com/article.php?articleID=138), but unfortunately only in Norwegian.

The calendar is an absolutely must-have asset for any future projects. I see several benefits:

- Good humour makes usability easy to understand
- Easy to understand for all project resources
- Visibility of usability in projects
- An actual calendar that can be used for simple scheduling (at least for some of the months that are not completely covered by popup-windows or similar!)

And I will also print a poster to decorate my manager's wall next to "[The importance of User Experience"](http://www.experiencedynamics.com/science_of_usability/usability_posters/user_experience/) poster by Frank Spillers. In my opinion the [best visual explanation](/2006/06/21/user-experience-revisited/) of user experience.
