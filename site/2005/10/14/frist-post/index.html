First post

Ok, let’s get right on it. This weblog is meant to deal with all aspects of usability. Here we will write about new and interesting technologies that hopefully will make usability easier to implement into your software or website. We will offer you our best tips and tricks and from time to time take up interesting projects and talk about our concerns and what our solution was.
