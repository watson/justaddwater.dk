Bad Usability Calendar 2007 Finally Ready

I’ve been waiting for this with excitement: Eidar and the rest of NetLife Research in Norway have finally released the 2007 version of Bad Usability Calendar.

Direct download: Bad Usability Calendar 2007 (1.2 MB PDF)

Very nice to see that the calendar actually is released under a Creative Commons license, so you can translate it into you native language as long as you keep the reference to NetLife Research.
