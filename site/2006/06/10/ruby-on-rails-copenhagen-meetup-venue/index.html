Ruby on Rails Copenhagen Meetup: Venue

We can now finally reveal the venue for the Ruby on Rails meetup in Copenhagen:

After a bid of research we have found the very central Café Selina in the central Copenhagen (just next to The Rock and “Gammel Torv”).

To sign up for the meetup and for detailed address info, see our original meetup post “Ruby on Rails Copenhagen Meetup”, which I’ve updated with all the details, including free beer.
