March 16, 2006
Thanks to Digg, del.icio.us and Lifehacker!
In the last 20 hours I’ve received 1.100 diggs, 1.533 bookmarks and over 16,000 unique visitors to my post, “Round-up of 30 AJAX Tutorials”. Thanks to all of the people who spent their free time to link this post. Additionally, special thanks to all of the helpful comments and user additions to the list!
