Elements of User Experience, Copenhagen, May 31st

Just a quick note: I’m attending Jesse James Garrett’s event in Copenhagen May 31st, “The elements of User Experience“. I’m really looking forward to this, as I’m a big admirer of Jesse and Adaptive Path.

You probably know that it was Jesse James Garrett who coined the term “AJAX”, which I think is a way to connect technology with valuable user experience.

Please drop me a note if you plan to attend. As always, I enjoy exchanging ideas and opinions around usability, web standards, Ruby on Rails and the web in general.
