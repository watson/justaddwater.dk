Bad Usability Calendar 2007 open for proposals

Our Norwegian friends at Netlife Research have opened for suggestions to next years Bad Usability Calendar. I already used the 2006 calendar with great impact in my professional engagements, and I’m really looking forward to next year’s version.

To make a calendar for 2007 that has the same level of unusual unusefulness, we would like your feedback.

Give us your best examples of bad usability!

The best idea will get an honourable mention, as well as a numbered limited edition of the 2007 Bad Usability Calendar. All suggestions that make the final cut will get a prize.

If you need inspiration, check out the Bad Usability Calendars for 2005(jpg), and 2006 (pdf). Thank you in advance for all your help.

Add your suggestions at their blog IAllEnkelhet.no (which means something like “in all it’s simplicity”).
