Criteria for the Capgemini Software Engineer Certification

It turns out i could not find any public web description of the certification I took last autumn while working with Capgemini.

I ended up at Level 2, which will have to meet all criteria from level 1 + the additional criteria from level 2.

So for convenience i will post all details here.

The table below is extracted from my certification sheet:

(bwt, i also have put the table below as a software engineer Certification Criteria)

Capgemini Certification criteria for Software Engineer, level 1-4 copied September 2011

.

.

 Level 1

.

 Expectations KPI

.

 1 Personal Profile

.

 Consulting Clearly explain issues, suggest way forward with client interaction

.

 Versatility and Vocation Demonstrate enthusiasm for Technology and S/W Engineering Personal Development in direction of level 2 SEC

.

 Technology Leadership not applicable

.

 2 SE Experience – Client Exposure

.

 Client Delivery Experience Successfully delivered within a client environment in one discipline Provide individual performance meeting/exceeding expectations which resulted in two successful engagements, or provided individual performance meeting/exceeding expectations which resulted in 200 or more days of a successful engagement (engagement not necessarily complete)

.

 Leadership and Management Basic experience Member of team with delivery ownership

.

 Sales and Account Contribution not applicable

.

 3 Skills and Contribution

.

 Structured Methods Received briefing Experience in at least 1 SE method (e.g. RUP, SLIM, Configuration Management, etc)

.

 Received fundamental training

.

 Is able to perform within project without the need for prior training

.

 Software Engineering Skills Masters 1 SE Discipline / Masters 1 programming language / Masters 1 vendor-specific technology Works without supervision in 1 SE Discipline. External Certification is "nice to have".

.

 Understands, at a high-level, the various SE disciplines and how they are interrelated

.

 Contribution to Community Member and active contributor of a Community of Practice (CoP). Contributed at least one piece of content to the CoP. Pre-requisite for the applicant to be a part of SE community on KM2.0.

Coaching candidate through the SE certification process
